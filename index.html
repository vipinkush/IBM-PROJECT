Strategic Project Plan

Clean Water & Sanitation: Lean Canvas

Structured business and execution model mapping out problems, target users, unique value propositions, and financial sustainability.

1
🚨 Top 3 Problems

Problem

  • Dangerous Contaminants: Groundwater & piped water frequently contains cholera, E. coli, arsenic, excess fluoride, and lead.
  • Zero Real-time Visibility: Water safety is tested infrequently in centralized labs; contamination is noticed only after hospitalizations occur.
  • Broken WASH Infrastructure: Water kiosks, borewells, and community toilets fall into disrepair due to no proactive maintenance tracking.
4
💡 Top Features

Solution

  • IoT Quality Node: Low-cost multi-sensor probe (pH, TDS, Turbidity, Flow) sampling water every 60 seconds.
  • Auto-Lockout Solenoid Valve: Shuts off dispensing instantly if toxic thresholds are breached.
  • Proactive Field App: Technicians receive automated alerts for filter replacements and toilet sanitation checklists.
8
📊 Progress Tracking

Key Metrics

  • Liters of verified safe drinking water dispensed daily.
  • Number of contamination incidents caught before consumption.
  • Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) broken water points (< 24h).
  • % reduction in reported waterborne illnesses in the cluster.
3
💎 Core Value

Unique Value Proposition

"An affordable, solar-powered IoT water filtration and real-time safety monitoring system that guarantees clean drinking water and alerts communities before illness spreads."

  • Eliminates blind consumption of unsafe water.
  • Visual green/red tap indicator understandable by anyone.
  • Zero dependence on distant testing labs.
9
🛡️ Hard to Copy

Unfair Advantage

  • Edge Drift Algorithm: Software filter that differentiates dirt spikes from hazardous chemical toxins.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Works via LoRaWAN/GSM with local SD card fallback when grid internet fails.
  • Grassroots Trust: Established village council (Panchayat) partnerships and transparent public indicators.
5
📢 Path to Users

Channels

  • Local government rural development departments and municipal utilities.
  • WASH NGOs (WaterAid, UNICEF) and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) grants.
  • School & community water kiosks ("Water ATMs").
  • Automated SMS/IVR voice updates to community members.
2
👥 Target Users

Customer Segments

  • Primary Beneficiaries: Rural and peri-urban households lacking treated municipal piped water.
  • Institutional Buyers: Public primary schools, rural healthcare clinics, and village councils.
  • Early Adopters: CSR grant managers (e.g. IBM Call for Code, tech foundations) seeking measurable, auditable impact.
7
💳 Main Expenses

Cost Structure

  • Hardware & Sensors: ESP32 controllers, probes (pH, TDS, Turbidity), UV-C lamps, and solenoid valves (~$25–35 / unit).
  • Cloud & Network: Cloud database telemetry ingestion (IBM Cloud / AWS), SMS alerts gateway.
  • Operations & Logistics: Field technician replacement filter cartridges, sensor calibration routines.
6
💰 Sustainable Model

Revenue Streams

  • Hardware Deployments: Funded via government water mission tenders and corporate CSR foundations.
  • Micro-Payment Water ATM: Nominal fee (e.g. $0.03 / ₹1 per 20L container) covering technician wages and replacement filters.
  • B2G Utility SaaS: Municipal subscription for centralized water safety compliance and contamination heatmaps.

Technical Architecture

How the System Works End-to-End

A 4-tier pipeline combining physical purification, edge sensory intelligence, cloud analytics, and rapid community action.

1

Multi-Stage Filtration

Raw water passes through sediment pre-filters, activated carbon, and UV-C sterilization chambers powered by solar panels.

2

Edge Sensor Node

An ESP32 microcontroller continuously reads analog inputs from pH, TDS, Turbidity, and flow sensors every 1.5 seconds.

3

Instant Local Action

If contamination is detected, the on-board relay closes the Solenoid Valve instantly, physically blocking contaminated water output.

4

Cloud & Community Sync

Telemetry syncs via Wi-Fi/GSM to cloud servers, broadcasting immediate SMS warnings to local families and dispatching repair teams.
